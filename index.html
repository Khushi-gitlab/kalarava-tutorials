ABOUT US

Welcome to Kalarava Tutorials

Academic support for students in Chitradurga.

Kalarava Tutorials, Chitradurga provides tuition and academic support for students from Class 1 to Class 10.

The programs include regular tuition for CBSE and State students, SSLC preparation, and coaching for Navodaya, Sainik and Murarji entrance examinations.

The programs also include regular tests, individual student attention, activity-based learning and parent meetings.
